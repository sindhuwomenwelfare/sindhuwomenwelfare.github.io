About Us

SINDHU WOMEN WELFARE SOCIETY NEWAI is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to empowering women, promoting education, supporting social welfare, and fostering inclusive community development. The society works towards creating opportunities, spreading awareness, and encouraging sustainable social progress through meaningful initiatives.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive, educated and empowered society where every woman has equal opportunities, dignity, confidence and the ability to contribute towards sustainable community development.

Our Mission

• Promote women empowerment through awareness and community participation.

• Encourage education and skill development for women and youth.

• Support social welfare, health awareness and environmental conservation.

• Work towards equality, self-reliance and sustainable development for all sections of society.
